Owner / Baseline Contract — isolated candidate v2

This contract does not install a formal trust root or grant maintenance authority.
Formal use remains BLOCKED until an Owner-approved public key fingerprint, Windows
SID, key storage provider and controlled receipt directory are pinned in the release
manifest. Never accept a public key supplied inside the same untrusted receipt.

Signed bytes: UTF-8 JSON.stringify(payload), preserving the stored property order.
Signature: Ed25519. Verifier receives the trusted public key separately.
The expected identity tuple must bind owner_sid, producer_pid,
producer_creation_date, epoch, candidate/manifest identity and request challenge.
ACK time must parse, not be future, and be within 30 seconds when accepted.
Quote and candle hashes must each be 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters.
Reject malformed time, wrong signature, wrong challenge, wrong expected identity,
nonzero queue/pending work or an unfrozen producer.

Receipt directory: protected Windows DACL; Owner SID, SYSTEM and Administrators.
No inherited broad write ACE. Verify owner/DACL after creation. Do not overwrite
formal ACL or provision a formal signing key as part of isolated acceptance.

Quiescence adapter:
1. Refuse new writes at the sole producer intake boundary.
2. Flush the original spooled store and wait for its original successful ACK.
3. Require no pending/inflight/queued rows and no persistenceGap.
4. Capture both files and independently hash the originals before/after copying.
5. Sign the exact epoch, challenge, producer identity, last ACK and both hashes.
6. Recover and independently verify immutable copies; do not claim continuity.

The isolated adapter uses unchanged production store/worker bytes. It is NOT yet
connected to the live Collector intake. A live producer not routed through this
boundary can still mutate cache; a fixture or adapter ACK cannot prove otherwise.
PREPARED / PREPARED_NOT_STARTED mean baseline preparation only.
CONTINUITY_UNKNOWN remains until the separately authorized continuity protocol
establishes and verifies the new epoch. Never reuse an old epoch as fresh proof.
