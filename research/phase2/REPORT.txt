Remaining blockers

2. Reader currently independently audits and hashes the entire bounded retained segment before extracting cursor delta. It is NOT an optimized growing-history reader. Logical read_bytes reports only manifest passes; audit and JSON reads add I/O. No end-to-end I/O reduction claim.
3. Quote changed-symbol decoding and exact existing merge/readthrough/liquidity mapping now work in quote-adapter.cjs. Seven additional tests verify known fields and changed-symbol subset parity against the same original full-cache mapping, 120s exclusion, missing-volume semantics and no implicit admission. Original priority list must be supplied explicitly; it is not recomputed. Quote publication/checkpoint remains blocked: no sink receipt/readback, previous DB quote snapshot identity or time-driven freshness re-evaluation binding exists. Other Writer stages (priority, source_status, daily volume, futures, snapshot, qualification, readback/lease) remain untouched and unverified.
4. Forming candles can become complete without another WS revision. No maturity scheduler is implemented. Publishing such rows blocks without advancing checkpoint rather than silently losing them.
5. Quality invalidation requires an explicit downstream invalidation/tombstone policy; mapping failure blocks rather than leaving a stale good row acknowledged.
6. Full fallback snapshot completeness is caller-provided and validated by fixed hash/key/date; real recovery reference/epoch binding remains unconnected.
7. Single offline caller only; no concurrent checkpoint owner/fencing. No DB transaction/readback, formal crash kill or natural trading data claims.
